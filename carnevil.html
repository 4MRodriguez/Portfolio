Artworks and Creative Projects

CARNEVIL CONCESSIONS - Ringling Creative Competition

    Prompt:

  • To create a cart that is realistic, functional, and recognizable, while still fitting in a themed environment.

    • Problem:

  • Being realistic with design ideas, while prioritizing guest experience and immersion.

    • Role: Concept designer

  • Designed a themed concession stand concept for the Ringling Theme Park Design Competition.
  • Developed a functional cart that incorporates storytelling.
  • Focused on creating creepy twists on classic carnival foods.

   
                 

    Process:

  • Researched vintage carnivals and creepy attractions.
  • Explored different layouts and guest interactions.
  • Refined the final concept using one-point perspective.
  • Considered functionality, storage, and service requirements.

         

Reflection:

-Learned how to combine storytelling with an operational design.

-Helped me become better at using creativity with physical constraints.

-Gained experience on presenting concepts.
