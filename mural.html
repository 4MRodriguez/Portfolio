Artworks and Creative Projects

Marine Biology Mural - Crooms Academy of Information Technology

    Prompt:

  • To create an immersive underwater themed mural that fit in a classroom setting.

    • Problem:

  • Translating an idea from paper to a large-scale surface.

    • Role(s): Concept designer and lead artist

  • Mocked up a rough design for the mural.
  • Painted octopus, whale, turtle, shark, jellyfish, a fish, and the seahorse.
  • Taught some people basics of acrylic painting.
  • Led a team of seven students.

   
                 

    Process:

  • Sketched out an initial design for general placement of the larger animals.
  • Drew animals on the wall for others to start painting.
  • Went back over bases made by other people for intricate details and realism.
  • Adapted to the expansion of the project over other walls per teacher’s request.

           

Reflection:

-Strengthened my collaboration and leadership skills.

-Better at making unified art piece with large team.

-Helped develop my ability to adapt to a changing project.
