Artist Statement

I design immersive environments with the intent of completely transporting guests, urging them to step out of reality and into a completely new world. I believe that a truly surreal setting must be felt, not simply seen. To achieve this, my creative process considers every aspect of an environment like a living story to enter. I craft spaces where every stimulus is considered, having it all work together to build a believable and cohesive world. To successfully create these places that transport people to another universe, I design for the entire range of human senses. Knowing that every guest interacts with space differently, I intentionally look beyond just the visuals. By layering sounds, textures, scents, and visual world-building, I ensure that my environments are not only atmospheric but also accessible to all. Ultimately, my goal is to work as a themed experience designer, manipulating space into stories that are a multi-sensory experience that create tangible, fantastical stories that welcome people in.
