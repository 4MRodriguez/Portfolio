Fanart - Naruto Themed Pencil Sharpener

    Prompt:

  • To create a functional, character-based object using no sculpting tools and limited, basic crafting materials.

    • Problem:

  • Working with limited materials to create a stylized yet functional pencil sharpener.

    • Role(s): Concept designer and artist

  • Handmade sculpture inspired by Gamatatsu from the anime Naruto.
  • Used basic materials (model magic, toothpicks, portable sharpener, toilet paper roll, tissues, tape, magnets, and acrylic paint).

     
                 

    Process:

  • Started with a sketch of how big I wanted parts to be.
  • Took sharpener apart and crafted place for shavings.
  • Modeled body with clay (used balled up tissues for a lighter head).
  • Painted finished model and sealed it.

       

Reflection:

-Put my problem-solving skills to the test.

-Helped me feel more comfortable using unconventional methods and materials to make a piece.
