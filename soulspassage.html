Soul's Passage

    Prompt:

  • To create an emotional story that allowed players to choose between continuing their journey on Earth or moving on to the afterlife in only nine weeks.

    • Problem:

  • Creating a cohesive visual novel game from scratch in nine weeks.

    • Role(s): Writer, visual developer, and 2D artist

  • Developed a branching narrative with nine endings.
  • Created all character art and UI assets.
  • Completed project in nine weeks.
  • Collaborated with two team members.

   
                 

    Process:

  • Mapped out script direction and planned specific endings.
  • Developed character designs (six total).
  • Made functional UI elements.
  • Created detailed cutscenes for each ending.

     

Reflection:

-Deepened understanding on crafting a narrative for interactive storytelling.

-Learned about putting focus on player experience.

-Better at creating detailed stories.
