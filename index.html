My name is Maria, and I am a recent high-school and college graduate earning an AA degree. I am currently attending UCF in the Immersive Experience Design to continue studying design and themed entertainment.

I have been creating art ever since middle school where I began teaching myself how to draw different cartoon and anime characters. From there, my art has expanded over many mediums and I have since been responsible for various events at my high school, which has shown me that creative ideas create memorable experiences.

Due to this, my goal is to work in themed entertainment design where I can craft environments that evoke emotion and encourage people to dream.
